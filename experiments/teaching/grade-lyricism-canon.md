## 4. What the high-grading anchors SHARE (lyricism → engine priors)
1. Period structure: balanced antecedent/consequent (answering pairs, ~4 bars).
2. Stepwise-dominant motion; one expressive leap per phrase that resolves/steps back.
3. Narrow range (~a 10th); cap range.
4. Breath as comma at phrase boundaries, never mid-word.
5. Single arc per phrase: rise then settle into the cadence (placed climax).
6. Speech-feel rhythm: anacrusis on entries, tied/sustained cadence notes, occasional broken rhythm — exactly what separates the A's from the mechanically-even C-band.
