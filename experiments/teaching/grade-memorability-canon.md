## 4. What the high-grading anchors SHARE (memorability → engine priors)
1. A compact rhythm-FIRST cell (≤4 notes) restated immediately.
2. Sequence / transposed repetition (not new contour).
3. A signature interval leap against stepwise context — the fingerprint.
4. A single, clearly-placed climax (one registral apex, reached by leap).
5. Distinctive rhythm: dotted/triplet/syncopated/odd-meter, not even eighths.
6. Chromatic color as a signature device (sparingly, motivic).
7. Tight cadential closure on a strong/held scale degree.

Synthesis: **economy + immediate repetition + one signature leap + one placed climax + non-uniform rhythm + clean close.** Controls fail by lacking 2–4 of these.
