# Canon tiers — selection axis: "memorable as UNSUNG instrumental music"

**STATUS: LOCKED by maintainer 2026-06-23** (option 1: stratify; ~5 controls;
honorable mentions promoted from the long tail). This tier list is the grading set.

Revised 2026-06-23 after maintainer review. The original canon (`docs/canon.md`)
was selected on **song-fame consensus**; entmotif needs **melodic memorability
that survives reduction to a bare, monophonic, lyric-less instrumental motif**.
So the primary axis here is: *was this composed to be memorable as instrumental
(unsung) music, and does it have a distinctive bare-melody identity?*

**Diagnostic used:** transcription confidence, with "thin melody" separated from
"couldn't source it." A tune whose identifiable phrase resists clean transcription
because there's little melody there (a chant, a one-note hook, a groove) fails the
test. A tune I under-graded only for lack of a public score does NOT.

Maintainer signals incorporated: film composers (Morricone, Mancini, Williams) =
clear winners; Tetris / Final Fantasy / Seven Nation Army = cohort misses (not
recognized); want **only 4–5 controls** + promote honorable mentions.
